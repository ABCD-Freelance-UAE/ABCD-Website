About

ABCD Freelance LLC‑FZ is run by Charles Boniface, a UK-trained mechanical engineer based in Dubai. His background spans design and test engineering on naval and automotive programmes, and hands-on project management — running test matrices, project plans and cross-department reporting from the shop floor up to leadership.

That combination of technical grounding and project delivery sits behind the company's four activities: structuring and holding interests, brokering deals, managing projects end to end, and providing mechanical engineering input where it's needed.

Outside the business, Charles has a long-standing motorsport career — a five-time karting champion and front-runner across UK national racing, now an ARDS Grade B instructor and MSUK certified driver coach at Yas Marina Circuit and Dubai Autodrome, alongside Goodwood Motor Circuit in the UK.
